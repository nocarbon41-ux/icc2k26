01 / ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

Why this
conference?

At a moment when AI is reshaping how we learn, make, interpret and communicate, the humanities provide the essential questions.

This conference brings scholars, researchers, educators, policymakers and industry experts together to examine artificial intelligence across language, literature, culture, communication, education and allied disciplines.

questions.

The conference takes place on 24 and 25 September 2026.

It is a hybrid conference, enabling both online and offline participation.

Students, research scholars, academicians, faculty, researchers, industry delegates, international delegates, authors and presenters are welcome.

Abstracts should be no more than 250 words.

A maximum of four authors is permitted. Full papers should not exceed five pages.

Email your abstract or full paper to aienglish2026@stcelr.ac.in.

There is no separate payment for publication. Any publication opportunity remains subject to editorial and peer-review processes.
